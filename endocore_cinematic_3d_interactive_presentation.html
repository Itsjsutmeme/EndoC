03 — IMMUNOSUPPRESSION Systemic Toxicity

Protects the graft only by shutting down the entire body's immune defense.

Infection & malignancy risk.

The real bottleneck: we can already replace the cells. We cannot protect them without suppressing the whole patient.

04 · THE SOLUTION — ENDOCORE BIOHYBRID SHIELD

An implant the size of an AirTag (28 mm × 3.5 mm). Carries CRISPR gene-edited beta cells behind a <30 nm nanoporous membrane, senses local tissue continuously, and releases a nanolitre of tolerogenic signal (IL-10 & TGF-β) exactly where an attack starts — and nowhere else.

Core Philosophy

Immunomodulation, not immunosuppression. Re-educating 1 cm³ instead of disabling the entire body.

01 — LOCAL, NOT SYSTEMIC

No whole-body immunosuppression. The tolerogenic dose stays confined to the 1 cm³ implant microenvironment.

02 — SENSES BEFORE IT ACTS

Four interstitial biomarkers (IL-6, IFN-γ, pH, CD8+ infiltration) scored on-device in under 50 ms.

03 — SEALED & RETRIEVABLE

Zero Wi-Fi, zero Bluetooth, zero cloud exposure. Power-only induction; removable via minor outpatient procedure.
